# SIG-CIS-Flavor

## SIG Introduction

**SIG-CIS-Flavor** is the openEuler Special Interest Group focused on serving users, developers, and partners in the Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS) region, including Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, and other member states.

The SIG aims to build and maintain a localized technical ecosystem around openEuler in the CIS region, with the following core objectives:

- **Streamline Upstream Contributions**: Simplify the process of committing packages, code, and scripts directly to the openEuler official community, removing the OpenScaler fork as an intermediary layer.
- **Standardise Tools & Procedures**: Develop unified contribution tools, rules, and procedures to lower barriers for individual and corporate contributors in the CIS region.
- **Enable Corporate Collaboration**: Establish a SIG‑based framework for joint solution development with enterprise users and technology vendors.
- **Execute Official Community Migration**: Lead the formal migration of existing OpenScaler community members and partners to native openEuler, consolidating efforts upstream.

The SIG serves as the official gateway between CIS‑based users, developers, and vendors and the openEuler upstream community.

## Scope of Work

The SIG's scope of work includes:

1. **Package & Code Contributions**: Migrate existing open‑source packages, dependency libraries, and toolchains from the CIS region to the openEuler official repository; maintain and submit patches and feature enhancements related to the local software and hardware ecosystem; integrate, maintain, and synchronize updates for the ISP RAS "Trusted Kernel" in openEuler.

2. **Technical Support & User Services**: Provide technical issue response and support for openEuler enterprise users in the CIS region; establish Russian/CIS‑language technical support channels (mailing lists, issue tracking, Telegram/MAX channels, etc.); collect and organize user requirements and feedback, reporting regularly to the TC and relevant SIGs.

3. **Localisation & Documentation**: Translate and maintain core openEuler documentation in Russian; write adaptation and deployment guides for CIS‑specific hardware and scenarios; continuously produce Russian technical blogs, tutorials, and advocacy content.

4. **Community Operations & Ecosystem Building**: Operate openEuler user communities in the CIS region (online/offline); organize regional technical events (meetups, webinars, hackathons, etc.); establish partnerships with universities, enterprises, and open‑source communities in Russia and the CIS region.
